Privacy Policy

How Zenova Study collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Who we are

Zenova Study ("Zenova", "we", "us") is an education platform for students in Nigeria. We run the website at zenovastudy.com.ng and the Study Hub at hub.zenovastudy.com.ng, where students can join classes, use study resources, take practice tests, and enter competitions.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and registration details: your name, email address, and the details you give when you create an account or register for a test, class, or competition (for example, the subjects you choose).
  • WhatsApp number: when you enter a competition with prizes, we ask for a WhatsApp number so we can contact you and pay your prize.
  • Payment information: payments for paid classes are processed by Flutterwave. We do not see or store your card details. We may receive confirmation of your payment, such as your name, email, and the amount paid.
  • Learning activity: your practice test answers, scores, rankings, and progress on the Study Hub.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email, phone, or WhatsApp.
  • Technical information: like most websites, our hosting providers record basic technical details such as your browser, device type, and the pages you visit.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and give you access to classes, tests, and resources.
  • To run competitions, rank participants, check winners, and pay prizes.
  • To confirm payments and give you access to the paid class you signed up for.
  • To answer your questions and send important updates about your account, classes, or competitions.
  • To fix problems and improve Zenova.

4. Rankings and leaderboards

If you take part in a competition or leaderboard, your name and score may be shown to other participants and published in the final rankings.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run Zenova, such as Flutterwave for payments and the companies that host our website and Study Hub.
  • Authorities, if the law requires us to.

Our classes and communities run on WhatsApp. What you post in a group can be seen by its members and is also covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

6. Students under 18

Many Zenova students are secondary school students. If you are under 18, please use Zenova with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. We collect only what we need to run our services. If you are a parent or guardian and want your child's information corrected or removed, contact us.

7. Cookies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and remember your settings.

8. How long we keep information and how we protect it

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no website or online service can be completely secure.

9. Your rights

Under Nigerian data protection law, you can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To make a request, email zenovaofficial188@gmail.com.

10. Links to other sites

Zenova links to other services such as WhatsApp, social media, and Flutterwave. We are not responsible for how they handle your information, so please read their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email zenovaofficial188@gmail.com or call 0707 360 7977. You can also read our Terms of Service.
