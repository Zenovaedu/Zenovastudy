Who We Are

Meet Zenova Study

Zenova Study helps students learn smarter and prepare more effectively through structured lessons, study resources, practice questions, and examination preparation — built to make learning simpler, more engaging, and more effective.

Our goal goes beyond passing examinations. We're building the knowledge, confidence, and consistency students need to succeed academically and beyond the classroom.

Learn. Practise. Improve. Achieve.
Mission & Vision

Making Quality Education More Accessible

Our mission is to make quality educational resources accessible to more students — a student's access to good education shouldn't be limited by their background. We envision Zenova becoming a trusted learning ecosystem for students across Nigeria and, eventually, beyond.
