Scheme of Work

Plan Your Study. Stay on Track.

Not sure what to study next? The Zenova Study Schemes of Work help you organize your preparation by giving you a structured overview of the topics you need to cover. Instead of studying randomly, use the scheme of work to understand what you need to learn, plan your study sessions, and track your progress.

Why Use a Scheme of Work?
01

Know What to Study

See the topics you need to cover across your subjects.

02

Stay Organized

Follow a structured learning path instead of studying randomly.

03

Track Your Progress

Mark topics as you complete them and see how far you've come.

04

Plan Your Revision

Identify topics you've completed and areas you still need to revise.

05

Prepare Consistently

Break your preparation into manageable topics and study sessions.
