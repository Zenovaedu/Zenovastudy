About Zen

Your Study Buddy at Zenova

Zen is the official mascot of Zenova Study. You'll find him in our classes, resources, and competitions, cheering students on as they prepare for their exams. Look out for the glasses, the navy Z hoodie, and the backpack full of books.

Study Smarter, Win Bigger

Zen's four habits for exam success.

  1. Set Goals

    Decide what score you are aiming for and what you need to do to reach it.

  2. Stay Focused

    Cut the distractions and give every study session one clear task.

  3. Learn Daily

    Short, regular study beats last-minute cramming.

  4. Keep Improving

    Check your results, find your weak spots, and work on them.
